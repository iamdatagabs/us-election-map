HOW TO READ THIS MAP

Margin, not just winner.

Margin is calculated as (Democratic votes − Republican votes) ÷ total reported votes × 100. Positive values indicate a Democratic advantage; negative values indicate a Republican advantage. The winner view shows which of the two major parties received more votes.

Data & limitations

Source: MIT Election Data and Science Lab, County Presidential Election Returns, 2000–2024. State values are aggregated from county-level returns in the project's versioned dataset. Reported votes are not the same as eligible-voter turnout. Missing observations are not imputed as zero; third-party votes remain in the total-vote denominator.
